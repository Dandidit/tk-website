Frequently Asked
Questions

Common questions about getting your books caught up.

How far behind can you help?
We regularly catch up businesses that are anywhere from a few months to several years behind on their bookkeeping.
How long does catch up take?
The timeline depends on the number of months, transaction volume, and the condition of your existing records.
What do I need to provide?
We generally need bank statements, credit card statements, invoices, receipts, and any existing accounting records.
Will catch up bookkeeping cost extra on top of my plan?
Catch-up work is quoted separately because the scope depends on the size and condition of your backlog.
What happens after I'm caught up?
Your reconciled books can be handed over to your accountant, or moved into an ongoing monthly bookkeeping plan.
