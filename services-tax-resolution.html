Frequently Asked
Questions

Common questions about tax preparation and filing.

Do you handle both personal and business tax filing?
Yes. We handle individual filings for sole proprietors and contractors, as well as business filings for partnerships and Sdn Bhd companies.
Do I need to be a TeraKira bookkeeping client to use tax resolution?
No. Tax resolution can be provided separately, although we may need to review your existing financial records first.
What if I'm behind on previous years' filings?
We can review the outstanding years, organize the required documents, and prepare a plan to bring your filings up to date.
How far in advance should I start?
It is best to begin before the filing deadline so there is enough time to review your records and resolve missing items.
What happens if LHDN has questions after filing?
We can help review the enquiry, prepare the supporting information, and explain the next steps required.
