FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with TeraKira — browse by category or search for your question.

What size of business do you work with?
Any size — from solo founders to growing SMEs. Our process and pricing scale with your transaction volume.
Will I have a dedicated bookkeeper?
Yes. You will work with one dedicated bookkeeper who becomes familiar with your business and accounts.
How do we get started?
Start by scheduling a consultation. We will review your needs, current records, and recommend the right service plan.
Do you work with businesses outside Kuala Lumpur?
Yes. Our bookkeeping services can be handled remotely for businesses throughout Malaysia.
How do I know which plan is right for my business?
It comes down to your monthly transaction count. Most solo founders start on Basic, while growing SMEs land on Grow. We'll confirm the right fit on your first call.
Can I switch plans as my business grows?
Yes, plan renews every year.
Do you handle LHDN tax filing?
We ensure the documents needed for LHDN tax filing are ready for your easy filing process.
What counts as a "transaction"?
Any money that comes in or out counts as a transaction.
What software do you use to complete my bookkeeping?
We use Quickbooks to ensure your books are in order.
Will I have a dedicated bookkeeper?
Yes. You will work with one dedicated bookkeeper who becomes familiar with your business and accounts.
Does TeraKira support both cash and accrual accounting?
Yes. We can maintain your books using either cash or accrual accounting, depending on your business requirements.
What happens to my financial data if I cancel?
Your financial records remain yours. We will help ensure that your available bookkeeping files can be transferred to you.
How often will I hear from my bookkeeping team?
You will receive regular monthly updates and can contact your bookkeeper whenever clarification is needed.
How far behind can you help?
We go as far as required as long as we have the information.
How long does catch up take?
The timeline depends on the number of months, transaction volume, and the condition of your existing records.
What do I need to provide?
We generally need bank statements, credit card statements, invoices, receipts, and any existing accounting records.
Will catch up bookkeeping cost extra on top of my plan?
Catch-up work is quoted separately because the scope depends on the size and condition of your backlog.
What happens after I'm caught up?
Your reconciled books can be handed over to your accountant, or moved into an ongoing monthly bookkeeping plan.
How many invoices are included in my plan?
Basic includes up to 20 invoices a month, Grow includes up to 100 with payment reminders, and Core includes unlimited invoice management and AR follow-up.
Can you send invoices directly to my clients?
Yes. We can prepare and send invoices directly to your clients using your business details and agreed payment terms.
How do payment reminders work?
Reminders can be sent before the due date, when an invoice becomes overdue, and again according to the agreed follow-up schedule.
What happens if a client still doesn't pay?
We flag the overdue balance, continue the agreed follow-up process, and inform you when further action may be required.
Does this integrate with my existing invoicing tool?
We can work with your existing invoicing process where possible, or help organize invoices through the tools used by TeraKira.
Do you handle LHDN e-Invoice compliance?
Yes. Eligible invoices can be generated and validated through LHDN's MyInvois system as part of the invoice workflow.
Do I need e-Invoice if my turnover is below RM1 million?
Your obligations depend on the current LHDN implementation rules and your business circumstances. We can review your position before setup.
Do you handle both personal and business tax filing?
Yes. We handle individual filings for sole proprietors and contractors, as well as business filings for partnerships and Sdn Bhd companies.
Do I need to be a TeraKira bookkeeping client to use tax resolution?
No. Tax resolution can be provided separately, although we may need to review your existing financial records first.
What if I'm behind on previous years' filings?
We can review the outstanding years, organize the required documents, and prepare a plan to bring your filings up to date.
How far in advance should I start?
It is best to begin before the filing deadline so there is enough time to review your records and resolve missing items.
What happens if LHDN has questions after filing?
We can help review the enquiry, prepare the supporting information, and explain the next steps required.
